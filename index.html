A World-Class Production

Vegas polish.
Grammy™ pedigree.

From the creative force behind The Docksiders — voted Best of Las Vegas and a headliner at 1923 Live inside The Venetian Resort — comes The 80s Revue: a world-class production built to celebrate the decade that defined pop culture.

Created by three-time Grammy™-nominated producer and entertainment executive Kevin Sucher, the show pairs an elite lineup of touring musicians and vocalists with the premium sound design, signature harmonies, and Vegas-level production value that have made Sucher's projects industry benchmarks.

Expect the biggest hits from Whitney Houston, Prince, Madonna, Michael Jackson, Gloria Estefan, Tears for Fears, Bon Jovi, and more — all delivered with the musicianship and showmanship audiences expect from a Kevin Sucher stage.

"The 80s Revue isn't just a concert — it's a full-scale experience."
